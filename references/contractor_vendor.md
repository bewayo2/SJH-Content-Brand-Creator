# Contractor and vendor rules

First determine whether the contractor is designing or producing approved artwork.

## Designing / creating
Examples: agency, graphic designer, web designer, signage designer, photographer.

Provide only the project-relevant brief, Brand Bible rules, visual guidance, approved logo/master files, relevant templates/specialist standards, verified names/facts and approval route. Contractors may develop solutions within the approved system but may not redefine the system.

## Producing / manufacturing approved artwork
Examples: printer, embroidery supplier, vehicle-wrap installer, fabricator.

Instruction: **Produce the approved artwork/specification exactly as supplied.**

Do not permit redrawing logos, changing typography, substituting colours by eye, altering layout/proportions, rebuilding stationery or creating alternate master artwork.

## Ownership and scope
Approved SJH assets are issued for a specific project. They are not redistributed, shared with other clients, reused outside scope or treated as vendor-owned assets. SJH branding remains the hospital's property.

## Vendor publicity
Do not assume a vendor may publish a case study, promotional post or other material referencing SJH. Such use requires specific hospital approval.

## Specialist contractors
Photography/video contractors must follow privacy, consent, role, PPE and facility-readiness rules. Signage/environmental contractors must also use the current approved technical signage/wayfinding standards rather than relying on the Brand Bible as a fabrication manual.
