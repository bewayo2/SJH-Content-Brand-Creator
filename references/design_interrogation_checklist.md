# Design interrogation checklist

Use this checklist silently before delivering designed output.

## Purpose
- What is the single most important thing this piece needs to do?
- Can the viewer identify that within a few seconds?

## Hierarchy
- Is the most important element unmistakably dominant?
- Is there a clear entry point?
- Is the reading order obvious?

## Typography
- Why this type size and weight?
- Have I used too many type treatments?
- Does this feel considered or default?

## Layout and spacing
- Is the grid doing real work?
- Are edges aligned cleanly?
- Is there enough breathing room?
- Is there a consistent spacing rhythm?

## Colour
- Is colour clarifying hierarchy or adding noise?
- Is the palette faithful to SJH?
- Is contrast adequate?

## Data and imagery
- Does each image earn its place?
- Is the chart type appropriate?
- Can the viewer tell what changed and why it matters?

## Craft
- Are repeated components truly repeated?
- Would a design director sign off on this?
- What is still generic?
