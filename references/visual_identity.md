# Visual identity

## Logo system
Use only approved master artwork. Core forms include the primary circular seal, secondary open seal, heritage horizontal lockup and compact horizontal wordmark. Fixed artwork must not be retyped, separated, repositioned, recoloured or independently recreated.

Never stretch, skew, crop, trace, screen-capture, rebuild, recolour or add effects to the logo. Do not create department logos. Do not extract the standalone symbol except where it is already incorporated into approved centrally issued artwork.

## Colour palette
Primary/support palette:
- Deep Blue `#2A57A3`
- Bright Green `#A8C947`
- Light Blue `#AECCEA`
- Pale Green `#E4EBCA`
- Pale Yellow `#FBF4A2`
- White `#FFFFFF`
- Light Grey `#F4F6F8`
- Medium Grey `#929697`
- Dark Grey `#4B5563`
- Near Black `#1F2937`
- Soft Neutral `#FBF8F2`

Alert colours are functional, not decorative. Bright green is an accent and should not be used as body text on white. Support colours are backgrounds, not body-text colours. Maintain accessibility contrast.

## Typography
- Aptos: default editorial/body typeface.
- Arial: approved production/system typeface where the approved template specifies it or cross-platform compatibility requires it.
- Aptos Display: approved secondary/display typeface for covers, major headings and designed material.
- Helvetica/generic sans-serif may follow Arial in web/email fallback stacks.
- Calibri is legacy fallback only where needed.

Do not change the typeface built into an approved master template. Avoid decorative fonts, long all-caps passages, tiny patient-facing text, condensed signage type and low-contrast text.

## Accessibility
Body text should meet at least 4.5:1 contrast; large display text at least 3:1. Do not rely on colour alone to communicate meaning.
