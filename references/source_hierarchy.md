# Source hierarchy and conflict handling

Apply sources in this order:
1. Current St. Jude Hospital Brand Bible.
2. Current approved specialist standard applicable to the task.
3. Current approved master template or master artwork for that application.
4. Current Visual Brand Guide.
5. Current Staff Brand Guide / quick reference.
6. Approved examples.

A lower source cannot override a higher one. Approved examples demonstrate precedent but do not create new rules. If a conflict affects public output, do not guess; identify the conflict and route it to the Communications Lead.

Separate stable brand rules from changing operational facts. Current contacts, service hours, service readiness, current office-holders, domains and similar facts require current verification.
