# Design philosophy

## Purpose over performance
Design should solve a communication problem first.

## Start plain, then elevate
Begin with the clearest functional expression of the content.

## Expertise with restraint
Strong design is not loud by default.

## Every element must justify itself
Typography, colour, spacing, layout, imagery, and graphics should all have a reason.

## Fewer moves, better executed
A small number of deliberate design moves is usually stronger than a page full of weak styling.

## References are teachers, not templates
Borrow principles, not surface styling.
