# Governance and approvals

The skill helps prepare work but does not grant institutional approval.

## Working approval roles
- Communications Lead — brand and tone; day-to-day custodian of the Brand Bible, templates, image library, website and social media.
- Department Head — material originating in the department within approved standards.
- Medical Director — clinical content and clinical voice.
- Nursing Director — nursing voice and nursing-led patient communication touchpoints.
- Quality Manager — quality, safety, regulatory and standards-related content.
- Chief Executive Officer or delegated executive — major public communication, institutional identity decisions, partner announcements, donor recognition and departures from the Brand Bible.
- Ministry/Government approval — where formal government communication, regulatory reporting or ministerial sign-off applies.

## Approval required before publication
Flag approval for:
- all public-facing material;
- media statements;
- signage;
- website pages;
- social media graphics/campaigns;
- patient information sheets;
- co-branded material;
- donor recognition;
- internal or external campaign material;
- new department/service/programme/unit names;
- major hospital-wide internal announcements.

Do not state that approval has been obtained unless the user provides evidence or the connected approval system confirms it.

## Departments
Departments may use approved templates and assets but may not create independent logos, unofficial colour schemes, separate taglines, alternate service names, unofficial social pages or unapproved public notices.

If the standard assets do not meet a genuine need, route the issue through the Communications Lead rather than creating an unofficial workaround.
