# Photography and imagery

SJH imagery should be grounded in the reality of the hospital and the community it serves.

Prioritise dignity, care, professionalism, truthful representation, real hospital environments where possible, staff competence, patient respect, community connection to southern Saint Lucia, current facility presentation, safety and cleanliness.

## Patients
Do not show identifiable patients without written consent for the specific use; patient records, charts, screens or private data; distressing clinical scenes; unsafe clinical practice; or children without appropriate consent.

## Staff
Show staff in roles appropriate to their actual work, with approved uniform/professional attire and correct PPE where required. Public use of staff imagery requires consent.

## Facilities
Facility imagery should be clean, organised, current, safe, accessible where relevant and welcoming. Do not show a service/area as operational before readiness is confirmed.

## Community
Prefer imagery reflecting southern Saint Lucia, families, public health, outreach, service, partnership and local trust. Avoid unrelated foreign hospital imagery in patient-facing/public material when it could be interpreted as SJH.

## Generated imagery
Generated imagery can support illustration/concept work but must not be presented as documentary evidence of the real SJH facility, actual patients/staff, actual equipment inventory, current services or operational readiness.
