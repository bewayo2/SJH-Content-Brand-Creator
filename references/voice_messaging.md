# Voice and messaging

The St. Jude Hospital voice is clear, calm, respectful, direct, warm, professional, practical, honest and reassuring.

Write with these rules:
- put the most important information first;
- explain what action the reader needs to take;
- use short sentences and everyday words;
- prefer active voice;
- use headings that help readers find information quickly;
- avoid unnecessary medical jargon and unexplained acronyms;
- avoid blame, speculation, political language and vague claims;
- do not overpromise;
- keep public communication factual, approved, timely and consistent.

Patient-facing language should be direct and respectful. Avoid administrative phrases such as “clients are hereby advised” and “failure to comply” when a clearer instruction is possible.

Working brand promise:
“St. Jude Hospital is here to provide safe, accessible, and compassionate care for southern Saint Lucia, supported by committed staff, modern systems, and a renewed hospital environment.”

Do not invent a corporate tagline. Distinguish heritage wording, the heritage lockup line, the fixed email-signature institutional line and working message directions.
