# SJH Content & Brand Creator

## Purpose

Use this skill whenever a person is creating, adapting, reviewing, or preparing material on behalf of **St. Jude Hospital**.

The skill is a **creation + compliance workflow**. It should help the user produce the actual material while applying the hospital's identity, language, visual, naming, accessibility, imagery, governance, and approval rules.

The skill does **not** replace institutional approval. It helps prepare work for the correct approval route.

## Authority and source order

Apply sources in this order:

1. The current approved **St. Jude Hospital Brand Bible** — highest authority.
2. Any current approved specialist standard supplied for the task (for example signage, digital, photography, clinical patient-information, or government standards).
3. The current approved master template or artwork for the specific application.
4. The current **Visual Brand Guide** — visual application reference.
5. The current **Staff Brand Guide / Quick Reference** — simplified staff reference.
6. Approved examples — evidence of application, not authority to invent a new rule.

If sources conflict:
- follow the highest applicable authority;
- do not silently reconcile competing rules;
- identify the conflict in **Creator / Approval Notes**;
- ask the Communications Lead for a decision when the conflict changes the output.

See `references/source_hierarchy.md`.

## Core operating rule: template first

Before designing or rebuilding anything, determine whether an approved SJH template or master asset already exists.

If an approved asset exists:
- use it;
- preserve its fixed branded elements;
- edit only fields intended to be editable;
- do not redraw or approximate the identity;
- do not create a parallel version because a different layout seems preferable.

If the approved asset cannot be found, direct the user to the Communications Lead or central asset library before creating a substitute.

Use `references/approved_assets.md` and `playbooks/create.md`.

### 3. Audience
- staff
- patients / families / caregivers

### 4. Content sensitivity
Identify whether the material contains:

## Universal content rules

Read `references/identity_naming.md` and `references/voice_messaging.md` when writing or reviewing text.

Always:
- use **St. Jude Hospital** on first reference;
- use **St. Jude Hospital, Saint Lucia** for external, regional, international, donor-facing, web, and search-facing material;
- use **SJH** only after the full name has appeared in the same item;
- do not use **St. Jude** alone in formal external communication;
- write clearly, calmly, respectfully, directly, warmly, professionally, practically, honestly, and reassuringly;
- put the most important information first;
- state the action the reader needs to take;
- use short sentences, everyday words, active voice, and useful headings;
- avoid unexplained acronyms, unnecessary jargon, blame, speculation, vague claims, political language, and unsupported claims;
- put patients and families first;
- do not overpromise;
- use approved names for departments, services, wards, units, programmes, and controlled locations.

## Patient-facing rule

Read `references/information_design_patient_communication.md` and the relevant playbook.

Patient-facing material should make it easy to understand:
1. what service is being discussed;
2. where the person needs to go;
3. what they need to bring;
4. what they need to do;
5. what will happen next;
6. who to contact;
7. what to do if symptoms worsen or plans change.

Do not insert medical advice, treatment instructions, contraindications, dosages, clinical pathways, or other clinical content that is not supported by an approved clinical source supplied for the task.

You may edit approved clinical content for clarity without changing its clinical meaning. If a change might alter clinical meaning, stop and require review by the appropriate clinical authority.

## Current-fact rule

Operational facts change more often than brand rules.

For these items, use a current verified institutional source or user-supplied confirmed information:
- telephone numbers;
- email addresses;
- website domains;
- addresses;
- service hours;
- service availability / readiness;
- opening or closure dates;
- department heads and current office-holders;
- current programmes;
- current social accounts;
- room / department / service names that are still provisional.

Use `references/verified_information.md`.

If a fact is not verified:
- do not guess;
- use a clear placeholder if the user wants a draft;
- list the item under **Items to verify before release**;
- do not make the public-facing artifact sound as if the fact is confirmed.

If the user explicitly supplies a fact for the draft, you may use it as user-supplied content, but do not independently label it “verified” unless a verified source supports it.

## Tagline and message rule

Do not invent, promote, or imply a new corporate tagline.

Treat these distinctly:
- **Historic phrase:** “Come share with us the service of giving love, care and hope to others.” Heritage use only.
- **Heritage lockup line:** “A Healthcare Provider of Excellence ~ Giving Love, Care and Hope to Others.” Part of approved master artwork; do not retype as a free-standing tagline.
- **Email-signature institutional line:** “Safe, Accessible and Compassionate Care for Southern Saint Lucia.” Fixed for the approved email-signature system; not a general-purpose campaign tagline.
- **Working message directions:** may guide approved communication but must not be elevated into a permanent tagline without approval.

## Visual identity rules

When a task includes visual design, read `references/visual_identity.md` and the current approved visual guide where available.

Never:
- redraw, screen-capture, trace, or approximate an SJH logo;
- stretch, skew, crop, recolour, retype, separate, or rearrange fixed logo artwork;
- add shadows, glows, outlines, or effects to the logo;
- create a department logo;
- create an unofficial colour scheme;
- create a department slogan or tagline;
- treat the standalone hospital symbol as a free design element unless it is already part of approved centrally issued artwork;
- use support colours as body-text colours;
- use alert colours decoratively;
- change the typeface built into an approved master template.

Use the approved palette and accessibility rules in `references/visual_identity.md`.

## Photography and imagery rules

Read `references/photography_imagery.md` when photographs, renders, illustrations, or generative imagery are involved.

Key rules:
- dignity, care, professionalism, truthful representation, and privacy come first;
- use actual SJH / southern Saint Lucia imagery where possible and appropriate;
- do not use unrelated foreign hospital imagery as if it represents SJH;
- do not show identifiable patients without the required written consent;
- do not show records, screens, charts, private information, unsafe practice, distressing clinical scenes, or minors without appropriate consent;
- show staff in roles, attire, uniforms, and PPE appropriate to the scene;
- show facilities as operational only when readiness has been confirmed;
- do not let generated imagery imply a real SJH facility, service, staff member, patient, or current operational capability when it is illustrative.

## Governance and approvals

Read `references/governance_approvals.md`.

The skill prepares work; it does not grant institutional approval.

At minimum, flag that approval is required before publication for:
- public-facing material;
- media statements;
- signage;
- website pages;
- social media graphics/campaigns;
- patient information sheets;
- co-branded material;
- donor recognition;
- campaign material;
- new department/service/programme/unit names;
- major hospital-wide internal announcements.

Also route content where applicable to:
- Department Head;
- Communications Lead;
- Medical Director;
- Nursing Director;
- Quality Manager;
- Chief Executive Officer / delegated executive;
- Ministry / Government approval.

Do not falsely state that approval has been obtained.

## Contractor/vendor behaviour

Read `playbooks/contractor.md` and `references/contractor_vendor.md`.

### Contractor is DESIGNING
Provide only the project-relevant rules, assets, templates, names, visual guidance, and approval route. They may design within the approved system but may not redefine the system.

### Contractor is PRODUCING/MANUFACTURING
Treat the approved production artwork/specification as fixed. The contractor should reproduce it as supplied, not reinterpret the brand.

## Review severity

When reviewing material, classify issues as:

### BLOCKER
Cannot responsibly proceed to publication until resolved.
Examples:
- exposed patient/private data;
- unsupported clinical instruction;
- unverified operational claim presented as fact;
- false or materially misleading institutional claim;
- unofficial/recreated logo where final production requires approved artwork.

### REQUIRED CHANGE
Clear conflict with SJH standards.
Examples:
- wrong naming;
- SJH used before the full name;
- unapproved colour treatment;
- stretched/cropped/recoloured logo;
- unofficial department identity;
- jargon-heavy patient instruction;
- body text too small for the defined application.

### RECOMMENDED
Improves clarity, consistency, accessibility, or brand quality but is not a definite violation.

### PASS
No material issue found against the available applicable sources.

A PASS is not institutional approval.

## Separate deliverable from internal notes

When creating outward-facing material, return two distinct layers:

### A. DELIVERABLE
Only the content/design intended for its real audience.

Do not insert:
- “this guide is intended to…”;
- design rationale;
- brand commentary;
- “moodboard-led” or other process language;
- approval instructions;
- placeholder explanations;
- notes to the creator;
unless those words genuinely belong in the final material.

### B. CREATOR / APPROVAL NOTES
Keep outside the artifact. Include only when useful:
- `Brand status:` PASS / REQUIRED CHANGE / BLOCKER / DRAFT — NOT YET REVIEWED
- `Approved asset/template:`
- `Approval required:`
- `Items to verify before release:`
- `Source conflict:`
- `Notes:`

## Creating files

When the user requests an editable or finished file:
1. identify the approved template/master first;
2. use the template if supplied/available;
3. preserve controlled brand elements;
4. use the application's required typography and palette;
5. insert only verified/user-confirmed operational facts;
6. review the rendered output, not only the source code;
7. run the applicable compliance review before returning the file;
8. provide creator/approval notes separately.

If the required approved template is not available, do not counterfeit it. Create content separately or, where Communications/design is explicitly creating a new controlled template, mark the work as a proposed design requiring approval.

## Final quality gate

Before returning any SJH material, check:
- correct institutional name;
- correct audience and channel;
- approved template/master used where one exists;
- no invented or unverified current fact presented as confirmed;
- no unsupported clinical content;
- voice and language compliant;
- patient instructions scannable and actionable where relevant;
- approved names used;
- logo treatment compliant;
- colours/typefaces/layout compliant;
- imagery/privacy/consent compliant;
- accessibility considered;
- approvals identified;
- creator notes excluded from the public artifact;
- final output visually inspected when a designed file was produced.
