# Patient information playbook

Use the approved patient-information template where available.

Patient-facing information should answer, where relevant:
- what service is being discussed;
- where the patient needs to go;
- what they need to bring;
- what they need to do;
- what will happen next;
- who to contact;
- what to do if symptoms worsen or plans change.

Write in plain language with short headings and one action per step. Use approved patient-friendly service names and 12 pt minimum body text for printed patient information.

Do not invent medical advice or alter clinical meaning. If a wording change may change clinical meaning, route it for clinical review.

Verify all current contacts, service hours, locations and access instructions. Patient-information sheets require approval before publication.
