# Document design specialist playbook

Use this playbook for decks, dashboards, reports, guides, brochures, patient information sheets, formal multi-page documents, and executive briefing materials.

## Required design-specialist behaviour
Silently consult:
- `references/document_design.md`
- `references/design_interrogation_checklist.md`
- `references/design_technique_catalog.md`
- `references/design_reference_library.md`
- `references/design_elevation_protocol.md`
- `references/design_philosophy.md`

## Process
1. Confirm the communication purpose and audience.
2. Determine whether an approved SJH template/master already exists.
3. Build the most functional version first.
4. Apply document structure, hierarchy, and accessibility.
5. Run the elevation protocol.
6. Interrogate typography, colour, layout, spacing, and data/imagery treatment.
7. Compare the design mentally against strong professional standards and refine further.
8. Deliver the polished result only.

## Output standard
The final output should feel hand-crafted, not auto-templated, while remaining aligned with the SJH Brand Bible and any approved templates.
