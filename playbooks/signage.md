# Signage playbook

The Brand Bible sets high-level signage rules but is not a complete fabrication manual. Use the current approved signage/wayfinding technical standard for production details.

High-level SJH rules:
- use approved department/service/location names;
- prioritise clear destination hierarchy, arrows and floor/zone information;
- use Aptos or Arial; avoid condensed/decorative fonts;
- keep destination lists left-aligned where practical;
- separate floor/location information visually from destinations;
- pair arrows and icons with words where needed;
- meet accessibility/contrast requirements;
- safety/regulatory signage standards take priority where they conflict with brand treatments;
- logo use is not required on every internal directional sign when the institutional context is already clear.

All signage, including temporary and contractor-installed signs, requires approval before installation. Contractors should manufacture approved drawings/specifications rather than reinterpret the brand.
