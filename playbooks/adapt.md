# Adapt workflow

Use when converting approved content from one channel or format to another.

1. Preserve verified facts and clinical meaning.
2. Reclassify the new audience/channel.
3. Apply the destination channel's naming, tone, length, hierarchy and visual rules.
4. Remove content that is unnecessary for the new channel, but do not add unsupported facts.
5. Keep calls to action and contact details clear.
6. Use the approved destination template/asset where one exists.
7. Re-run approval requirements because a new channel may trigger a different approval route.

Adaptation is not permission to invent a new tagline, service claim, department name or visual system.
