# Photography playbook

Before planning or approving photography, identify whether the scene includes patients, staff, minors, clinical areas, sensitive information, facilities or community settings.

Check:
- required consent for the specific use;
- no patient records/screens/private information visible;
- correct staff role, attire and PPE;
- no unsafe or distressing clinical presentation;
- facility is clean, organised, current and accurately represented;
- service/area is shown as operational only when readiness is confirmed;
- community imagery reflects southern Saint Lucia where appropriate.

Do not use unrelated foreign hospital photography as if it depicts SJH. Generated imagery may be illustrative but must not imply documentary truth about the real facility, patients, staff, equipment or current services.
