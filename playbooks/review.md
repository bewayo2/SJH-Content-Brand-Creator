# Review workflow

Review the item against the highest applicable SJH sources.

Check:
- institutional naming and approved service/department names;
- voice, clarity, plain language and unsupported claims;
- current operational facts;
- clinical/safety content;
- approved template/master use;
- logo treatment;
- colour and typography;
- accessibility and information hierarchy;
- photography/privacy/consent;
- co-branding/vendor treatment;
- required approvals.

Classify each finding as:
- **BLOCKER** — cannot responsibly proceed until resolved;
- **REQUIRED CHANGE** — clear conflict with SJH standards;
- **RECOMMENDED** — improvement, not a definite violation;
- **PASS** — no material issue found against available applicable sources.

A PASS is not institutional approval. Give the user precise corrections rather than generic criticism.
