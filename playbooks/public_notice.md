# Public notice playbook

Use the approved public-notice template where available.

Structure:
1. St. Jude Hospital identity.
2. Clear notice title.
3. Affected service or audience near the top.
4. Publication/effective date where relevant.
5. Essential information in plain language.
6. Clear action required.
7. Contact or next step.

Use the appropriate alert colour only when the message is genuinely informational, cautionary, urgent or confirmatory. Alert colours are functional, not decorative.

Verify service hours, closures, openings, locations, contact details and readiness claims before release.

Public notices require approval before publication. Keep creator notes and approval instructions outside the public-facing notice.
