# Memo and correspondence playbook

Use the approved letterhead or internal memorandum template where one exists. Do not rebuild stationery.

For internal memos, make clear:
- what has changed;
- what is confirmed;
- what is not yet final;
- what staff should expect next;
- what action is required;
- who owns the action;
- where to escalate issues;
- where support is available.

Use St. Jude Hospital on first reference, approved department/service names, clear subject lines and direct language. Verify dates, contacts, office-holders and operational details.

Preserve fixed template elements, including logo placement, typography and branded header/footer treatments. Identify any required Department Head, Communications, clinical, quality or executive review separately from the memo itself.
