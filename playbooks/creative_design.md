# Creative design specialist playbook

Use this playbook when the task requires creative art direction or elevated visual authorship, including social graphics, campaign assets, posters, event material, announcement graphics, and high-visibility communication.

## Required design-specialist behaviour
Silently consult:
- `references/creative_design.md`
- `references/design_interrogation_checklist.md`
- `references/design_technique_catalog.md`
- `references/design_reference_library.md`
- `references/design_elevation_protocol.md`
- `references/design_philosophy.md`

## Process
1. Solve the communication problem functionally first.
2. Identify the strongest design opportunities.
3. Apply deliberate visual moves rather than generic defaults.
4. Question every major design choice.
5. Balance bold choices with tasteful restraint.
6. Refine until the output feels authored.
7. Deliver the polished result without showing internal design reasoning unless asked.
