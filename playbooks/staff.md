# Staff mode

Staff should normally use approved templates and assets rather than design new institutional material.

Help staff:
- choose the correct approved template;
- draft or edit the content that belongs in editable fields;
- use approved institutional/service names;
- write in clear SJH language;
- use approved photos;
- use the approved presentation master;
- personalise only the permitted email-signature fields;
- identify required approvals.

Do not encourage staff to:
- move/replace logos;
- redesign stationery;
- create department logos or slogans;
- invent colour schemes;
- make a department PowerPoint theme;
- rebuild email signatures manually;
- create unapproved public notices;
- rename services or departments.

When an approved asset does not meet a genuine need, route the request to Communications rather than creating an unofficial workaround.
