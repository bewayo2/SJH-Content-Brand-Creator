# Press release playbook

Use the approved press-release template. Do not rebuild its header or brand treatment.

Use **St. Jude Hospital, Saint Lucia** for formal external communication and introduce the full institutional name before SJH.

A press release should:
- lead with the most important verified fact;
- state dates, locations and service details accurately;
- avoid speculation, blame, political language and unsupported claims;
- use approved department/service names;
- keep quotations attributable and approved;
- include only current verified contact details;
- distinguish confirmed operational information from planned/future activity.

Media/public communication requires approval before release. Route clinical, quality, executive and government content to the appropriate authority where applicable.
