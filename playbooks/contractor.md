# Contractor / vendor mode

First determine the contractor's role.

## Designing / creating
Provide the project brief plus only the relevant approved rules, master assets, names, verified facts, visual guidance, specialist standards and approval route. The contractor may design within the SJH system but may not redefine it.

## Producing / manufacturing
Treat approved production artwork/specifications as fixed. Instruct the vendor to reproduce exactly as supplied. No redrawing logos, substituting colours, changing fonts, rearranging layout or creating alternate master artwork.

## Photography/video
Apply consent, privacy, correct-role, PPE, facility-readiness and truthful-representation requirements.

## Signage/environment
Use the current signage/wayfinding technical standard in addition to the Brand Bible. Do not treat the Brand Bible alone as a fabrication specification.

## Scope
SJH assets are supplied for the specific engagement and remain hospital property. They are not redistributed or reused outside scope. Vendor publicity/case studies referencing SJH require specific approval.
